PDFUnlock desktop distribution
=============================

PDFUnlock source code is available under the MIT license:
https://github.com/zajanmy/PDFUnlock

This executable bundles unmodified third-party components. Their licenses are
separate from PDFUnlock's MIT license. Package metadata and supplied license
files are included in the executable and extracted with its runtime files.
